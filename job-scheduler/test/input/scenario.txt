# Job Scheduler demo script
# SCHEDULE <jobId> <PRINT|FAIL> <delayMillis> [intervalMillis]

# --- One-time jobs ---
SCHEDULE job1 PRINT 100
SCHEDULE job2 PRINT 250
STATUS job1
WAIT 500
LOG
STATUS job1
STATUS job2

# --- A one-time job that fails: reschedule logic must not touch it again ---
SCHEDULE job3 FAIL 100
WAIT 300
LOG
STATUS job3

# --- A recurring job: let it fire a few times ---
SCHEDULE job4 PRINT 100 150
WAIT 600
LOG
STATUS job4

# --- Cancel the recurring job, confirm it stops producing new EXEC lines ---
CANCEL job4
WAIT 400
LOG
STATUS job4

# --- Cancel a job well before it's due: should succeed and never fire ---
SCHEDULE job5 PRINT 500
CANCEL job5
STATUS job5
WAIT 700
LOG

# --- Duplicate job id: job1 already exists (and stays in the registry after completion) ---
SCHEDULE job1 PRINT 100

# --- Unknown job id errors ---
CANCEL unknown-job
STATUS unknown-job

SHUTDOWN
