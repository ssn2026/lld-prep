# Notification System demo script

SUBSCRIBE Alice EMAIL
SUBSCRIBE Alice SMS
SUBSCRIBE Bob PUSH

# --- normal priority: PlainContent + SignatureDecorator only ---
NOTIFY Alice NORMAL Welcome Thanks for signing up!

# --- high priority: PlainContent + SignatureDecorator + UrgentPrefixDecorator ---
NOTIFY Alice HIGH ServerDown The database is unreachable

# --- Bob is only on PUSH, so only 1 channel gets notified ---
NOTIFY Bob LOW WeeklyDigest Here is what you missed this week

# --- Carol never subscribed to anything: legal, just notifies 0 channels ---
NOTIFY Carol NORMAL Hello This should reach nobody

UNSUBSCRIBE Alice SMS
NOTIFY Alice HIGH FollowUp SMS channel should no longer receive this

# --- graceful no-op: unsubscribing from a channel that was never subscribed ---
UNSUBSCRIBE Bob SMS

# --- error: missing body -- Builder validation fires ---
NOTIFY Alice NORMAL EmptyBody

# --- error: unknown channel type ---
SUBSCRIBE Alice FAX
